UX Research & Design Project — Ticket App Study

Google UX Design Certificate capstone · Completing November 30, 2026

The project

A usability study of a ticket app design: plan the research, run an unmoderated study, synthesize what participants did and said into themes and insights, prioritize the fixes (P0/P1/P2), and revise the prototype — then fold the results back into the case-study deck.

Process

  • Study plan — research questions, tasks, and method set before recruiting.
  • Unmoderated study — five planned participants; four responses in, final response pending.
  • Synthesis — affinity diagramming to group observations into themes; insights grounded in real participant data.
  • Prioritization — insights ranked P0/P1/P2 so the prototype revision tackles what matters most first.
  • Revision & retest — prototype updated from the prioritized insights and retested with the same five tasks.
